Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This is a private, internal tool operated by Ümmügülsün Türkmen (TurkmenApps). It is used only by its owner to upload her own marketing videos to her own TikTok and Instagram accounts and to read those accounts' public performance statistics.

What data is accessed

Basic profile information, account statistics and video statistics of the owner's own accounts, and the videos the owner chooses to upload.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data of other users is collected.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings; local tokens are deleted on request.
